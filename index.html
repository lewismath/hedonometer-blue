Method

Happiness is the average LabMT score of words in each day's posts, ignoring neutral words (scores 4–6). Entropy rate is estimated with ProcessEntropy on a fixed 1M-word random sample per day.

Privacy & data

Only aggregates are published. No posts, handles or account identifiers ever leave the research server. Download the daily data (CSV) · all data files.
